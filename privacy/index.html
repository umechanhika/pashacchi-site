Pashacchi Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Last revised: October 27, 2026 (sections 3 to 5 now cover the iPhone version)

Pashacchi does not collect photos or personal information. The app does not connect to the internet, so your photos and how you use the app never reach the developer.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect any personal information about users or children. There is no mechanism for receiving names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, or usage data.

2. Information kept only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent off the device.

  • Photos you take (kept inside the app until a parent picks them)
  • The date and time each photo was taken, and brightness values used to detect failed shots
  • App settings, and the number of photos saved to the photo app in the current month

No location information is recorded in photos. Photos that have not been picked yet are deleted if you uninstall the app.

3. When photos leave the app

Photos leave the app only when a parent does one of the following.

  • Saves picked photos to the device's photo app
  • Sends picked photos to another app, such as FamilyAlbum, using the share feature

Once handed over, photos are handled under the terms and privacy policy of the receiving app. If your photo app backs up to a cloud service, such as Google Photos or iCloud Photos, saved photos are handled under that service's terms.

4. Permissions we use

  • Camera: to take photos.
  • Notifications: to remind you when there are photos you have not picked yet.
  • Add to Photos (iPhone only): to save picked photos to the Photos app. This permission only adds photos; the app cannot see the photos already in your library.
  • Run at startup (Android only): to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Google Play Billing (Android only): for the purchase that removes the save limit.

The app does not use permissions for internet access, location, or reading photos or contacts.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iPhone. Payment information is not shared with the developer and is handled under Google's or Apple's privacy policy.

6. Ads and analytics

The app contains no ads and no tools that analyze how you use it.

7. Children's privacy

Pashacchi is designed to be used by children. It does not collect personal information from children. Purchases and settings are available only from the screens for parents.

8. This website

Section 6 is about the app. This website (pashacchi.com) uses Google Analytics by Google and Microsoft Clarity by Microsoft to learn how it is visited and to improve it. They use cookies and similar technologies to collect data such as pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate region, and how the page is scrolled and tapped. These data are sent to Google and Microsoft and handled under their policies: Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

These tools run only after you choose “Accept” in the cookie notice shown on your first visit. If you choose “Decline”, nothing is loaded and no cookie is set. Your choice is kept in your browser, and you can change it any time from Cookie settings at the bottom of each page. The app itself sends nothing to this website or to these services.

The video on the home page is hosted on YouTube (Google) and is loaded from YouTube only when you press play. Playing it sends a request from your device to Google, handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Until you press play, nothing is loaded from YouTube.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and add a revision date below the effective date.
