Lock your phone to the camera before handing it to your child

Updated October 27, 2026 · About 5 minutes

On Android, turn on a screen lock and app pinning. On iPhone, turn on Guided Access. Locking the screen to the camera is done by the phone itself. Set it up first, and your child can't leave Pashacchi or open anything else.

Go to the steps for your phone: Android · iPhone

On Android

1. Set a screen lock on the phone

Search the Settings app for "screen lock" and set a PIN, pattern, or fingerprint. If you already have one, go straight to the next step.

Without a screen lock, the app pinning protection in the next step does nothing.

2. Turn on app pinning and ask for the PIN before unpinning

Search the Settings app for "app pinning" and turn it on. On some phones it is called "screen pinning". The setting sits deep in the menus and the path differs by phone, so searching gets you there faster than browsing.

On the same screen, turn on "Ask for PIN before unpinning". The exact wording also differs a little by phone.

3. Start shooting in Pashacchi

Open Pashacchi and follow the welcome screens to the camera. The phone asks whether to pin the screen; allow it. From then on, the phone stays locked to the camera while shooting.

If your child unpins the screen with the phone's buttons, steps 1 and 2 make the phone ask for your unlock code right there. Without it, no other app opens.

On iPhone

1. Turn on Guided Access

Search the Settings app for "Guided Access" and turn it on. It is under Accessibility.

On the same screen, open Passcode Settings and set the passcode for ending Guided Access. You can also use Face ID or Touch ID.

2. Open the camera, then start Guided Access

Open Pashacchi and go to the camera. Then triple-click the side button (or the Home button on iPhones that have one) and tap Start. From then on, the phone stays locked to the camera while shooting.

Until Guided Access starts, the camera screen shows "Not locked" at the top left. While it does, a swipe up from the bottom of the screen leaves the app.

3. End Guided Access

Triple-click the side button (or the Home button), enter your passcode, and tap End. While Guided Access is on, other apps such as Photos can't open.

When you want the phone back

This works the same on Android and iPhone. Hold the small button in the top-right corner for 3 seconds and answer the multiplication shown on screen. That takes you to the picking screen. Three wrong answers in a row lock the input for a while.

While you're at it

  • If the shutter sound is a problem where you are, turn it off in Pashacchi's settings on Android. On iPhone, the app can't turn it off.
  • Notifications pull your child's attention away, so silence them on the phone while it's handed over.
  • If you'd rather not hand over your own phone, see how to turn an old phone into a kids' camera.
  • If you're still deciding whether to buy a toy camera instead, read Before you buy a kids' camera: toy camera or your phone?

Don't have Pashacchi yet?

It runs on phones and tablets with Android 10 or later, and on iPhones with iOS 17 or later. The basics are free.
