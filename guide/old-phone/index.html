Turn an old phone into a kids' camera

Updated October 27, 2026 · 3 min read

The Android phone or iPhone left over from your last upgrade makes a kids' camera, with the SIM taken out. Pashacchi never goes online, so you need Wi-Fi only to install it and to buy the upgrade. After that it shoots anywhere. If the phone gets dropped or lost, your own phone is safe. The steps take about 15 minutes, a little more if you reset the phone.

1. Check the version

On Android, open the Settings app and look under "About phone" for the Android version. 10 or later works. Pashacchi can't be installed on Android 9 or earlier.

On iPhone, open the Settings app and go to General, then About, for the iOS version. 17 or later works. iPhones older than the iPhone XS and XR can't run iOS 17, so they can't use it.

If an update is waiting, install it first.

2. Reset the phone if you want to

If old apps and data on the phone bother you, search the Settings app for "reset" and do a factory reset. Move anything you want to keep, like photos and contacts, off the phone first.

You don't have to reset. If you skip it, uninstall the apps you don't want your child to touch.

3. Connect to Wi-Fi and sign in to the store with your own account

A connection is needed only to install from the store. Connect to Wi-Fi, then sign in to Google Play with your own Google account on Android, or to the App Store with your own Apple account on iPhone.

Using your own account means that if you buy "Remove the save limit", the purchase carries over to your other devices on the same account. It doesn't carry over between Android and iPhone.

4. Lock the phone to the camera

These keep your child inside the camera. The steps are in Lock your phone to the camera before handing it to your child. On Android, that's a screen lock and app pinning. On iPhone, it's Guided Access. Set them up even on an old phone.

5. Install Pashacchi and follow the welcome screens

Install Pashacchi, open it, and follow the welcome screens. On Android, the phone asks whether to pin the screen when the camera opens; allow it. On iPhone, start Guided Access once the camera is open.

6. Hand it over. You pick the photos later

From here on, the phone can be offline or in airplane mode. New photos wait inside Pashacchi, and only the ones you mark "Keep" are saved to that phone's photo app.

To see the kept photos on your own phone, do one of these:

  • Turn on Google Photos or iCloud Photos backup on the old phone. The next time it's on Wi-Fi, the photos show up in the same app on your phone too.
  • Install FamilyAlbum on it and use "Send to FamilyAlbum" in Pashacchi to send them in one go. On iPhone, tap "Share" and pick FamilyAlbum.

Common worries

  • The old phone has little storage. There is no limit on how many photos can be taken, but when free space runs low, the app stops shooting and takes you to the picking screen. Pick, free up space, and shooting resumes.
  • The battery is worn. The screen stays on while shooting, so the battery drains. Charge before you go out, and bring a power bank for a long day.
  • Is anything missing without a SIM? Not in Pashacchi. It has no feature that uses calls or data.
  • Would a toy camera be better? It depends on your family. The points to compare are in Before you buy a kids' camera: toy camera or your phone?
