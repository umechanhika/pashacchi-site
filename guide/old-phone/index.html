Turn an old phone into a kids' camera

Updated October 20, 2026 · About 15 minutes, a little more if you reset the phone

The Android phone left over from your last upgrade makes a kids' camera, with the SIM taken out. Pashacchi never goes online, so you need Wi-Fi only to install it and to buy the upgrade. After that it shoots anywhere. If the phone gets dropped or lost, your own phone is safe.

1. Check that it runs Android 10 or later

Open the Settings app and look under "About phone" for the Android version. 10 or later works. If an update is waiting, install it first.

Pashacchi can't be installed on Android 9 or earlier.

2. Reset the phone if you want to

If old apps and data on the phone bother you, search the Settings app for "reset" and do a factory reset. Move anything you want to keep, like photos and contacts, off the phone first.

You don't have to reset. If you skip it, uninstall the apps you don't want your child to touch.

3. Connect to Wi-Fi and sign in to Google Play with your own account

A connection is needed only to install from Google Play. Connect to Wi-Fi and sign in with your own Google account.

Using your own account means that if you buy "Remove the save limit", the purchase carries over to your other devices on the same account.

4. Set a screen lock and turn on app pinning

These keep your child inside the camera. The steps are in Lock your phone to the camera before handing it to your child. Turn both on, even on an old phone.

5. Install Pashacchi and follow the welcome screens

Install Pashacchi from Google Play, open it, and follow the welcome screens. When the camera opens, the phone asks whether to pin the screen; allow it.

6. Hand it over. You pick the photos later

From here on, the phone can be offline or in airplane mode. New photos wait inside Pashacchi, and only the ones you mark "Keep" are saved to that phone's photo app.

To see the kept photos on your own phone, do one of these:

  • Turn on Google Photos backup on the old phone. The next time it's on Wi-Fi, the photos show up in Google Photos on your phone too.
  • Install FamilyAlbum on it and use "Send to FamilyAlbum" in Pashacchi to send them in one go.

Common worries

  • The old phone has little storage. There is no limit on how many photos can be taken, but when free space runs low, the app stops shooting and takes you to the picking screen. Pick, free up space, and shooting resumes.
  • The battery is worn. The screen stays on while shooting, so the battery drains. Charge before you go out, and bring a power bank for a long day.
  • Is anything missing without a SIM? Not in Pashacchi. It has no feature that uses calls or data.
