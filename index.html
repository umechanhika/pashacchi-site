How it works

Hand it over. Pick. Keep.
That's all.

  1. 1

    Hand it to your child

    Tap "Start camera" and pass the phone. Your child shoots as much as they like.

  2. 2

    You pick

    When you have a moment, mark the photos to keep. View them one by one, or swipe across the list to select many at once.

  3. 3

    Keep them where they belong

    Only the photos you picked are saved to the same place as your usual camera. The rest are deleted at once. Send them straight to FamilyAlbum, too.

Features

Fun for kids.
Easy for parents.

  • Capture your child's face, too

    The back and front cameras shoot at the same time and are combined into one photo. You keep what your child saw and the look on their face. On supported phones, it's on from the start.

  • Failed shots are sorted out for you

    Very dark photos and photos that are almost one color are gathered at the end of the list. You only pick from the ones that came out. Nothing is deleted without you.

  • Pick quickly, one big photo at a time

    View each photo large and tap "Keep" or "Don't keep" to move on. Pinch to zoom and check for blur.

  • Send straight to FamilyAlbum

    Hand your saved photos over to the FamilyAlbum app in one go. Only the photos you picked reach grandparents.

  • Kids can't open other apps

    The screen stays pinned to the camera while shooting. To go back home, press and hold the small button in the top-right corner for 3 seconds and answer a multiplication question. Reading digits isn't enough to get through.

  • A gentle reminder to pick

    If photos are still waiting, you get a reminder 1 day and 7 days after the last shot. It won't keep nagging.

Privacy

Family photos
stay on your phone.

This app has no server. Photos are held only on your phone.

  • Never goes online

    The app has no ability to connect to the internet at all. Neither your photos nor any usage data is sent anywhere.

  • No ads

    There are no ads and no outside links on the screens your child touches. Nothing to tap by mistake.

  • No location in photos

    Where a photo was taken is never recorded in it. Share it to FamilyAlbum or social media and your home or park stays private.

  • Only camera and notifications

    No access to your photo library, contacts or microphone is requested. The only photos that leave the app are the ones you choose to save or send.

FAQ

The questions people ask first.

Which phones does it work on?

Phones and tablets running Android 10 or later. iPhone and iPad are not supported. Shooting with the back and front cameras at the same time works only on supported phones. The welcome screens tell you whether yours is one of them.

Can my child get back to the home screen on their own?

The screen is pinned to the camera while shooting. To get out, press and hold the small button in the top-right corner for 3 seconds, then enter the answer to the multiplication shown on screen. Three wrong answers in a row lock the input for a while. For extra safety, turn on "Ask for PIN before unpinning" in your phone's settings, so that even if the system buttons are used to unpin, your PIN or fingerprint is required.

Where are the photos saved?

Photos waiting to be picked are held inside the app only. The ones you choose to keep are saved to the same place as your usual camera app, so they show up in Google Photos and other backups. The rest are deleted at once.

What happens if I uninstall the app or switch phones?

Photos you haven't picked yet are lost when the app is uninstalled, and they don't move to a new phone. Pick and save them first. Saved photos carry over like any other photo from your camera app.

Will my phone run out of space?

There is no limit on how many photos can be taken in one go, but when free space runs low the app stops shooting and takes you to the picking screen. Reminders 1 day and 7 days after the last shot help you keep up.

Can I turn off the shutter sound?

Yes, in the settings. Handy next to a napping sibling or in quiet places.

Do I need FamilyAlbum?

No. If FamilyAlbum isn't installed, the "Send to FamilyAlbum" button simply doesn't appear, and your picked photos go to your usual photo app.
